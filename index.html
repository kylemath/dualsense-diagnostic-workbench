Macro Close-Up: The Wiper Springs, Alignment Notch & `88..155` Carbon Track
Crucial Inspection Step
Once you unclip the green `RX` door, here is an actual macro close-up of the three internal pieces side-by-side:
• 1 (Blue Circle): The white vertical D-shaft post on the joystick gimbal.
• 2 (Gold & Blue): The removable white wiper disc. The Gold oval shows the ultra-fine twin brass wiper springs that accumulate black oxidized carbon gunk; the Blue rectangle shows the keyed notch that must slide vertically onto the gimbal post.
• 3 (Red Oval): The `12 o'clock` top center of the black carbon horseshoe (`88..155`), where the wiper rests 95% of the time. Scrub this exact arch with a 90%+ IPA Q-tip!
What to Look For With the Door Hinged Open (Left Photo): 1. Notice how the white wiper disc stays on the metal gimbal post when you first hinge the green door open.
2. Check the top arch of the black ring inside the green door: Wipe it firmly with an alcohol-soaked Q-tip. If a black streak comes off onto the Q-tip and the track underneath looks uniformly matte black, it's cleanable!
3. Check for bare white scratches: If the black carbon coating at `12 o'clock` is scraped down to bare white plastic, the resistive film is worn through and you can swap the sensor/stick.
4. Re-tension the brass springs: Gently lift the top curved brass wiper fingers outward by `~0.2mm` with tweezers so they press firmly against the cleaned carbon track, align the vertical notch onto the white post, and click the green door shut!
Anatomy of the Right Stick `RX` Potentiometer & the `88..155` Skip Zone
Zero-Solder Serviceable
Each thumbstick has two clip-on green/teal Alps RKJXV potentiometers: the side sensor controls Y-Axis (`RY`), and the bottom/front sensor controls X-Axis (`RX`). Inside `RX`, a removable white rotor disc carries twin brass wiper fingers across a black horseshoe carbon track.
Step-by-Step Illustrated Disassembly & Potentiometer Opening
Photos: iFixit (CC BY-NC-SA)

Step A (Unclip Front Black Trim & L1/R1 Bumpers): Pry the black trim from the bottom handle tips upward, then pop off L1 & R1 to access the 4 shell screws (2 under L1/R1, 2 at bottom of handles).

Step B (Remove 4 Screws, Unplug Battery & Flip Board): Remove the 4 outer screws, unclip the rear shell, unplug the battery (pull the white plug, not the wires), remove the 1 battery tray screw, and flip the board to pull off the right thumbstick cap.

Step C (How to Open the Green `RX` Door WITHOUT Desoldering!): The 3 metal legs at the bottom of the green potentiometer are made of thin, bendable sheet metal that acts like a bottom hinge while still soldered to the motherboard! Slide the tip of your tweezers into the top seam between the metal joystick cage and the green housing, pop the two top plastic latches loose, and tilt the green housing outward ~30° (just enough to pull the white wiper disc out with tweezers and slide an IPA Q-tip inside). Do not bend it past 45° so the legs don't fatigue—when done, push it right back against the cage until both top latches click shut!
